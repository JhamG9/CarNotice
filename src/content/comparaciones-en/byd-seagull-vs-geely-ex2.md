This compares the versions sold in Colombia: the BYD Seagull GL 300 and the Geely EX2 Max. The Seagull is also known as the Dolphin Mini in other Latin American countries.

The EX2 is 4,135 mm long and the Seagull, 3,780 mm; the EX2 has 5 seats and the Seagull, 4. The wheelbase is 2,650 mm against 2,500 mm. That translates into a trunk of 375 liters plus 70 up front, against 230 liters for the Seagull, and into room for rear passengers. The Seagull weighs 1,165 kg and the EX2, 1,300 kg.

### Range and power

Both spec sheets include NEDC: 395 km (245 miles) for the EX2 and 300 km (186 miles) for the Seagull, 95 km (59 miles) more. The EX2 is also rated at 325 km (202 miles) WLTP. The battery explains the distance: 39.4 kWh against 30.1 kWh. For anyone who leaves the city often, that margin decides whether charging on the way is needed.

The EX2 has rear-wheel drive and delivers 85 kW (114 hp), with 11.5 s from 0 to 100 km/h. The Seagull, with front-wheel drive, delivers 55 kW (74 hp) and takes 13.9 s. The 2.4 s difference is noticeable when merging onto a fast road.

### Charging

The EX2 accepts 70 kW on DC and goes from 30 to 80% in 21 minutes; the Seagull accepts 30 kW and takes 30 minutes over the same stretch. Adding 50 points to a bigger battery in less time is a clear advantage on a trip. The EX2 uses a CCS2 connector and the Seagull GB/T, which means checking station compatibility.

### Equipment and safety

The EX2 has six airbags, autonomous braking, adaptive cruise control, a 360° camera, a 14.6-inch screen and a wireless charger. The Seagull has four airbags and a 10.1-inch screen, and lists those aids as not included. Both have V2L. The EX2's warranty is 6 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles); the Seagull's, 5 years.

### Who each one is for

The EX2 suits people who need five seats, more trunk space and a car for the highway. The Seagull works for people who use it in the city, on short trips, and want the lightest and most compact car. This comparison does not take price into account.
