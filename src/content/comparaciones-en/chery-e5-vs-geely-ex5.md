This compares the entry versions sold in Colombia: the Chery E5 Comfort (model year 2026) and the Geely EX5 SE (2026). They are two compact electric SUVs with front-wheel drive, a single motor and an LFP battery. Prices are not considered in this comparison.

### Almost identical range, with more battery in the Chery

On the WLTP cycle, the E5 is rated at 350 km (217 miles) and the EX5 at 345 km (214 miles), a gap of 5 km (3 miles) that changes no decision in practice. The E5 gets there with 50.6 kWh and the EX5 with 49.5 kWh, so the Chery's lead is one of capacity, not efficiency. The EX5 declares a consumption of 15.9 kWh per 100 km; the E5's spec sheet lacks that figure, so there is no way of knowing which one uses less.

### Charging

Both accept 100 kW or more on DC: 110 kW for the E5 and 100 kW for the EX5. Even so, the EX5 goes from 30 to 80% in 20 minutes and the E5 needs 30 minutes for the same stretch. On a long trip, those 10 extra minutes per stop are the most tangible difference between the two. For slow charging, the E5 declares 6.6 kW on AC; the EX5 has no figure.

### Performance and space

The EX5 is quicker: 6.9 s from 0 to 100 km/h against 7.1 s for the E5, and 160 kW (215 hp) against 155.1 kW (208 hp). On space, the E5 has a 442-liter rear trunk plus 32 liters up front, and the EX5 has 461 liters at the back with no front trunk recorded; in practice the E5 adds up to 474 liters and the EX5 stays at 461.

### Safety and equipment

The E5 declares 7 airbags against 6 for the EX5. The EX5, however, has a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, a figure the E5's spec sheet does not include. Neither declares autonomous braking, adaptive cruise or lane keeping as standard in these trims. The EX5 also declares ventilated seats, a panoramic roof, a heat pump, wireless charging and V2L, which powers devices from the battery. The E5's spec sheet only records no wireless charging and has no data on the rest.

### Who each one suits

The EX5 suits people who fast-charge on the road and value the published crash test and comfort equipment. The E5 suits people who prioritize one more airbag and slightly more total cargo capacity, and who charge mainly at home or on short trips. This comparison does not take price into account.
