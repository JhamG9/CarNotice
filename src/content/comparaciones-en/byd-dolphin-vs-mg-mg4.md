This comparison uses the European specs of the BYD Dolphin 60.4 kWh and the MG MG4 Premium Long Range. They are two compact hatchbacks of almost the same length (4,290 mm and 4,287 mm), with LFP batteries and a single motor, so the decision comes down to details of efficiency and charging.

The Dolphin has 60.4 kWh, 150 kW (201 hp) and front-wheel drive. The MG4 has 64 kWh, 140 kW (188 hp) and rear-wheel drive. The BYD goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 7.3 s and the MG in 7.5 s: the gap is small, even though the Dolphin has 10 kW (13 hp) more power.

### Range and consumption

The MG4 is rated at 452 km (281 miles) WLTP; the Dolphin, 427 km (265 miles). That 25 km (16 mile) advantage comes from its larger battery, not from efficiency: the Dolphin uses 15.9 kWh per 100 km and the MG4, 17.6 kWh. Put another way, the BYD makes better use of each kilowatt-hour, and the MG makes up for it with capacity. Weight helps explain it: 1,658 kg for the Dolphin against 1,765 kg for the MG4.

### The stop at the charger

Fast charging is what separates them most. The MG4 accepts up to 154 kW and goes from 10 to 80% in 25 minutes. The Dolphin reaches 110 kW and needs 42 minutes. On a road trip, that 17-minute difference repeats at every stop, and for anyone who often charges away from home it counts for more than the extra 25 km (16 miles) of range.

### Trunk, warranty and equipment

The MG4 offers 388 liters of trunk space against 364 for the Dolphin. Both have a 360° camera, heat pump, heated seats, blind spot monitor and automatic braking, and both have V2L. The MG4 adds a wireless phone charger, which the Dolphin lacks.

The warranties make a contrast: MG gives 7 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles) on the car and the same on the battery. BYD gives 6 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles) on the car, but 8 years or 250,000 km (155,000 miles) on the battery, considerably more battery coverage.

This comparison does not take price into account. The MG4 suits people who travel and need short charging stops. The Dolphin suits people who use the car mainly in the city and value lower consumption and the longer battery warranty.
