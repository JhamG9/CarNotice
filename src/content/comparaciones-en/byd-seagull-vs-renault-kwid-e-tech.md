This comparison uses the versions sold in Colombia: the BYD Seagull GL 300 and the Renault Kwid E-Tech Techno. They are two small four-seat EVs competing for buyers who want a second car for the city. The Seagull is also known as the Dolphin Mini in other Latin American countries.

On range, the spec sheets use different cycles: 300 km (186 miles) NEDC for the Seagull and 225 km (140 miles) WLTP for the Kwid. NEDC gives higher figures for the same car, and converted to an estimated WLTP equivalent the Seagull's 300 km comes to about 248 km (154 miles). That is a 23 km (14 mile) lead on an approximate figure, so in practice they perform similarly. The battery can be compared directly: 30.1 kWh against 26.8 kWh, 3.3 kWh more for the Seagull, a real margin though not a huge one.

### Size and trunk

The Kwid is shorter (3,701 mm against 3,780 mm), narrower (1,580 mm against 1,715 mm) and weighs 977 kg against 1,165 kg. On a narrow street or in a tight garage, that matters. The Seagull does have a longer wheelbase (2,500 mm against 2,423 mm), yet its 230-liter trunk falls short of the Kwid's 290 liters. For shopping, the Kwid carries more.

### Power and charging

The Seagull delivers 55 kW (74 hp) and 135 Nm, and goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 13.9 s. The Kwid delivers 48 kW (64 hp) and 113 Nm, and takes 14.6 s. The gap is small: in the city they feel similar. For fast charging, both accept 30 kW on DC: the Seagull from 30 to 80% in 30 minutes, the Kwid from 20 to 80% in 45. The stretches do not match, but the spec sheets show the Kwid takes longer per battery point recovered. The Seagull uses a GB/T connector and the Kwid CCS2. On AC, both accept 7 kW.

### Safety and equipment

The Kwid has six airbags, autonomous emergency braking and lane keeping; the Seagull has four airbags and neither of those two aids as standard. The Seagull, on the other hand, has V2L and the same top speed (130 km/h). The Seagull's warranty, 5 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles), beats the Kwid's 3 years and 100,000 km (62,000 miles), and the battery warranty is 8 years on both, with 150,000 km (93,000 miles) for the Seagull and 120,000 km (75,000 miles) for the Kwid.

### Who each one is for

The Kwid suits people who prioritize trunk space, active safety and the smallest size. The Seagull suits people who want more battery, power and warranty. This comparison does not take price into account.
