TikTok is full of videos of EV owners showing a COP 150,000 electricity bill against COP 400,000 fill-ups. The comparison is real, but incomplete: energy savings are only part of it, and the purchase price weighs more than everything else combined.

This is the full calculation for a driver covering **12,000 km (7,500 miles) a year in Bogotá**, about 1,000 km (620 miles) a month.

## Energy: the biggest day-to-day difference

| Item | EV, charged at home | Gasoline, 40 km per gallon (25 mpg) |
|---|---|---|
| Reference price | COP 864 per kWh (residential tier 4, Enel, August 2026) | COP 16,331 per gallon (Bogotá, October 2026) |
| Consumption | 16.5 kWh per 100 km, with charging losses | 25 gallons per 1,000 km |
| Cost per kilometer | **COP 142** | **COP 408** |
| Annual cost | **COP 1.71 million** | **COP 4.90 million** |

The difference is about **COP 3.2 million a year**. If the gasoline car gets 35 km per gallon (22 mpg), which is normal in traffic jams, the saving rises to about COP 3.9 million.

Two warnings. If you almost always charge at fast charging stations, the EV's cost per kilometer can rise above COP 210 and the saving drops by about half. And the price of gasoline in Colombia is no longer frozen: in October 2026 the government reversed a COP 46 per gallon increase, but the decision only covers that month.

## What you save outside the tank

- **Vehicle tax:** by law it cannot exceed 1% of the commercial valuation on an EV. In Bogotá, new private EVs also get a 60% discount for five years. On a COP 100 million car, that leaves the tax at about COP 400,000, when a gasoline car of equal value pays several times more.
- **SOAT:** Law 1964 of 2019 orders a 10% discount on the premium for EVs on this mandatory accident insurance.
- **Technical inspection:** there is a discount for EVs, and they do not pay for the emissions test.
- **Maintenance:** no oil changes, engine filters, spark plugs or timing belt. Brakes last longer thanks to regenerative braking.
- **Pico y placa:** in Bogotá, paying to skip these driving restrictions, the so-called solidarity pico y placa, costs COP 70,294 a day in 2026. For anyone who paid it often, this is the biggest saving on the list.

## What works against it

- **Purchase price:** it remains the deciding factor. If the EV costs COP 20 million more than its gasoline equivalent, energy savings take about six years to pay back the difference.
- **Comprehensive insurance:** EV premiums are usually higher, because body repairs involving batteries and sensors are expensive. Get a quote before signing.
- **Home charger:** installing a wall unit costs about COP 3 million according to the utility Enel.

> The rule of thumb: the EV clearly wins when its purchase price is similar to the gasoline car you were looking at and you can charge at home. If either condition fails, run the numbers yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to recover the investment in an EV?

Divide the price difference by the annual saving. With a COP 20 million difference and COP 3.2 million in energy savings, it is about six years. Adding vehicle tax, SOAT and maintenance shortens the term.

### Are the savings the same in Medellín or Cali?

No. The energy tariff and the price of gasoline change by city, and each department or municipality sets its vehicle tax discounts.

### Does a hybrid save the same?

No. A hybrid still uses gasoline on every trip. It saves against a conventional car, but considerably less than an EV.
