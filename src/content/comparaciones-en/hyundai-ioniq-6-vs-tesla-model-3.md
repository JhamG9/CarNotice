This comparison uses the European specs of the Hyundai IONIQ 6 63 kWh RWD and the Tesla Model 3 RWD, model year 2026. Both are rear-wheel-drive, five-seat sedans, but they start from different ideas: the Hyundai favors charging speed and the Tesla, power and efficiency.

The IONIQ 6's strong point is recharging. It accepts up to 195 kW on DC and goes from 10 to 80% in 18 minutes, six minutes less than the 24 of the Model 3, which accepts 175 kW. On an 800 km (497 mile) trip with two stops, that difference adds up to 12 minutes. Its 63 kWh NMC battery is somewhat larger than the Tesla's 60 kWh LFP.

The Model 3 still goes further. Its WLTP range is 572 km (355 miles) against 521 km (324 miles) for the Hyundai, and consumption explains it: 12.2 kWh per 100 km against 13.4 kWh. The 51 km (32 mile) gap shows on long stretches with no charger in sight.

The widest distance is in the motor. The Hyundai delivers 125 kW (168 hp) and goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 8.3 s; the Tesla delivers 208 kW (279 hp) and does it in 6.2 s. With 125 kW the IONIQ 6 is enough for city and highway, but the Model 3 overtakes with more margin. Top speed differs too: 185 km/h for the Hyundai and 201 km/h for the Tesla.

The Hyundai is longer (4,925 mm against 4,720 mm) and has a longer wheelbase (2,950 mm against 2,875 mm), but its trunk is smaller: 401 liters against 594, and the front trunk drops from 88 to 45 liters. For a family, the Tesla carries more despite being shorter overall.

On equipment, the IONIQ 6 has Android Auto, Apple CarPlay and V2L; the Model 3 offers none of the three. The Tesla, on the other hand, has a heat pump and blind spot monitor, and the Hyundai's spec sheet says it has neither. A heat pump helps preserve range in cold weather. Warranty: 5 years with no mileage limit for the Hyundai, 4 years or 80,000 km (50,000 miles) for the Tesla.

The IONIQ 6 suits people who travel with frequent stops, use CarPlay and value a warranty with no mileage cap. The Model 3 suits people who want more range, more acceleration and more trunk space. This comparison does not take price into account.
