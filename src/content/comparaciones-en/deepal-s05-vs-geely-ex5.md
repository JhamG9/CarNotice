These are the entry versions sold in Colombia: the Deepal S05 Max (model year 2026) and the Geely EX5 SE (2026). Both are compact SUVs, but the S05 is a range-extended EV, with a 1,497 cc gasoline engine that extends its range, and the EX5 is purely electric. That changes what each range figure means.

### Range

The EX5 is rated at 345 km (214 miles) on the WLTP cycle with 49.5 kWh. The S05 is rated at 158 km (98 miles) of electric range on the NEDC cycle and 1,100 km (684 miles) combined with gasoline, with a 27.3 kWh battery and a 51-liter tank. The EX5 also has an NEDC figure, 410 km (255 miles), and that one is comparable: on electricity alone, the EX5 covers 252 km (157 miles) more. The S05 makes up for it with the gasoline engine, which removes dependence on a charger on long trips.

### Charging

The S05 fast-charges from 30 to 100% in 60 minutes; the EX5 goes from 30 to 80% in 20 minutes, with a 100 kW maximum. The measured stretches differ and cannot be divided into each other. What does show is that the EX5 is designed to recharge on the road, while the S05 uses its tank so it does not have to.

### Performance and space

Power is almost the same: 159.6 kW (214 hp) for the S05 and 160 kW (215 hp) for the EX5. Acceleration does change, 7.5 s against 6.9 s from 0 to 100 km/h, and the EX5 weighs 185 kg less (1,662 kg against 1,847 kg), since the S05 also carries a gasoline engine and a tank. The S05 has rear-wheel drive and the EX5 front-wheel drive. The trunk is 464 liters in the S05 and 461 liters in the EX5, a practical tie.

### Safety and equipment

The S05 declares 6 airbags, autonomous braking, adaptive cruise, lane keeping, blind spot warning and a 360° camera. The EX5 declares 6 airbags and a 360° camera, but not those other four assistants as standard. Its advantage is a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, a figure the S05 does not have.

### Who each one suits

The S05 suits people who travel long distances, do not want to plan recharging and value more standard driver assistance. The EX5 suits people who get around mainly in the city, charge at home or at work, and prefer a pure EV that is lighter and has a published crash test. This comparison does not take price into account.
