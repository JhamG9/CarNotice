This compares the versions sold in Colombia: the GAC AION Y Plus Full Equipo (model year 2025) and the Geely EX5 SE (2026). Both are compact electric SUVs with front-wheel drive, an LFP battery and a single motor. The question is whether the AION's bigger battery makes up for what the EX5 does better.

### Battery and range

The AION carries 63.2 kWh and the EX5 49.5 kWh, 13.7 kWh more capacity. The only cycle they share is NEDC: 490 km (304 miles) for the AION and 410 km (255 miles) for the EX5, an 80 km (50 mile) lead. The EX5 also declares 345 km (214 miles) on WLTP, a cycle for which the AION has no figure. On consumption, the AION reports 13.9 kWh per 100 km and the EX5 15.9 kWh, so the AION uses less energy per kilometer and its lead does not depend on battery size alone.

### Charging

Both accept up to 100 kW for fast charging. The EX5 goes from 30 to 80% in 20 minutes; the AION takes 40 minutes from 10 to 80%. Because the stretches start at different percentages they cannot be matched, but the EX5 is the one offering shorter stops with a smaller battery. For slow charging, the AION reports 6.6 kW; the EX5 has no figure.

### Performance and space

The EX5 wins here: 6.9 s from 0 to 100 km/h against 8.5 s, with 160 kW (215 hp) and 320 Nm against 149.1 kW (200 hp) and 225 Nm. The AION tops out at 150 km/h and the EX5 reaches 175 km/h, a limit you notice on the open road. On space the EX5 wins narrowly, with a 461-liter trunk against 405 liters and 4,615 mm of length against 4,540 mm.

### Safety and equipment

Both spec sheets declare 6 airbags, ISOFIX and a 360° camera, and neither has autonomous braking, adaptive cruise or lane keeping. The EX5 also declares 5 Euro NCAP stars, ventilated seats, wireless charging, a heat pump and V2L, which powers devices from the battery; the AION's spec sheet records no wireless charging and has no data on the rest. The AION answers with a general warranty of 160,000 km (99,000 miles) against the EX5's 150,000 km (93,000 miles).

### Who each one suits

The AION Y Plus suits people who prioritize range and low consumption with the bigger battery. The EX5 suits people who value acceleration, the larger trunk, the published crash test and comfort equipment. This comparison does not take price into account.
