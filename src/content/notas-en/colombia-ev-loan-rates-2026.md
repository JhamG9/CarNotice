Banks have found a niche to compete in with EVs, and the difference in rates is large. According to a May 2026 analysis by the Colombian business daily Portafolio, financing a hybrid or EV in Colombia can cost **about 40% less in interest** than a gasoline car.

## Market rates

| Type of car | Rate range (effective annual) |
|---|---|
| Combustion | 23.5% to 26.5% |
| Hybrids and EVs | 12% to 16.6% |

These were the figures reported by lender. Rates change every month: confirm them with the lender before signing.

- **Bancolombia (Sufi):** about 12.5% effective annual for EVs.
- **Davivienda:** around 15.25% effective annual.
- **Finandina and BBVA:** between 16% and 16.65% effective annual.

## The math: COP 80 million over 60 months

We calculated the monthly payment and total interest on a five-year COP 80 million loan, with no insurance or other charges:

| Effective annual rate | Monthly payment | Total interest |
|---|---|---|
| 12.5% (EV, low rate) | COP 1,772,945 | COP 26.4 million |
| 15.25% (EV, mid rate) | COP 1,872,994 | COP 32.4 million |
| 16.6% (EV, high rate) | COP 1,922,422 | COP 35.3 million |
| 23.5% (combustion, low rate) | COP 2,177,512 | COP 50.7 million |
| 26.5% (combustion, high rate) | COP 2,289,332 | COP 57.4 million |

Between the best EV rate and the best combustion rate there is **a difference of about COP 24 million in interest**. That is more than the reduced VAT saves on many entry-level cars.

## What to check besides the rate

1. **Mandatory loan insurance.** Life insurance and comprehensive car insurance are added to the payment and can change the final cost considerably. On EVs, comprehensive cover is usually more expensive.
2. **Green rate conditions.** Some apply only to certain brands, partner dealers or minimum down payments.
3. **Fixed or variable rate.** If the policy rate of the Banco de la República, Colombia's central bank, falls, a variable-rate loan could get cheaper; if it rises, more expensive.
4. **Prepayments.** Confirm that you can pay down principal without a penalty, for example with the VAT refund from the DIAN, Colombia's tax authority.

> A useful idea: if you plan to request the [VAT refund on the car](/es/notas/devolucion-iva-carro-electrico-dian-paso-a-paso/), plan to use that money to pay down principal. It reduces the interest over the whole loan.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the lowest rate for an EV?

In May 2026, the lowest reported was close to 12.5% effective annual at Bancolombia. Rates change often, so get quotes from several lenders.

### Do hybrids also get a green rate?

Yes, most green loan lines include hybrids, although some lenders give better terms to EVs.

### Does leasing suit me?

For self-employed individuals or companies, leasing can have tax advantages. Compare it with a traditional loan with an advisor's help.
