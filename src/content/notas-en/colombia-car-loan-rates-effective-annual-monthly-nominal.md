Car loan offers in Colombia mix ways of stating the rate that cannot be compared at a glance. A dealer says "from 0.99% a month", a bank publishes "12.5% EA" and a third talks about "11.84% nominal". They are practically the same rate. Understanding the difference avoids a bad choice and is the basis for comparing the [real cost of a loan](/es/notas/costos-ocultos-credito-vehicular/).

## The three formats

**Effective annual (EA, for *efectiva anual*).** This is what the money really costs in a year, including interest charged on interest. It is the form in which the Superintendencia Financiera, Colombia's financial regulator, certifies the usury rate, and the best one for comparing.

**Monthly in arrears (MV, for *mes vencido*).** This is the rate applied each month to the balance. It is the one brands usually advertise because the number is small.

**Nominal monthly (NMV).** This is the monthly rate multiplied by 12. It sounds annual, but it is lower than the effective annual rate because it leaves out the effect of compounding.

## The formulas

To go from monthly to effective annual: **EA = (1 + MV)¹² − 1**

To go from effective annual to monthly: **MV = (1 + EA)^(1/12) − 1**

The nominal rate is simply **NMV = MV × 12**.

## The most common mistakes

**Multiplying the monthly rate by 12 and thinking that is the annual rate.** 1.2% a month is not 14.4% a year: it is 15.39% effective annual. On a COP 72 million loan over five years, believing the first when it is the second hides almost COP 2 million in interest.

**Comparing an EA rate with a nominal one.** If one bank offers 14% EA and another 13.5% nominal monthly, the second is more expensive: 13.5% NMV is equivalent to 14.37% EA.

**Comparing rates without looking at total cost.** Two loans at the same rate can cost different amounts because of insurance, fees and application charges. Always ask for the **total monthly payment**, with everything included, and the **total amount payable** over the loan.

**Confusing fixed and variable rates.** A rate tied to the IBR, a Colombian benchmark, or another indicator can rise if the rate of the Banco de la República, the central bank, goes up; in September 2026 it moved to 12.25%. We explain this in [this article](/es/notas/banco-de-la-republica-tasa-1225-credito-carros/).

> There is only one question to ask the advisor: what is the effective annual rate and how much will I pay in total? With those two figures you can compare any offer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does EA mean on a loan?

Effective annual: the real cost of money over a year, including compound interest.

### What is 0.99% a month in effective annual terms?

12.55% effective annual.

### Which rate is best for comparing loans?

The effective annual rate, because it is the same measure the Superintendencia Financiera uses and it does not hide compounding.
