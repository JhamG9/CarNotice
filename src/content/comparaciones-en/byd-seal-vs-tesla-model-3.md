This compares the European specs of the BYD Seal 61.4 kWh RWD Comfort and the Tesla Model 3 RWD, both model year 2026. They are rear-wheel-drive sedans with LFP batteries of almost identical capacity, 61.4 kWh for the BYD and 60 kWh for the Tesla. That leaves the comparison with a single question: what each one does with the same energy.

To begin with, the Model 3 goes much further. Its WLTP range is 572 km (355 miles) and the Seal's, 460 km (286 miles), 112 km (70 miles) less with 1.4 kWh more battery. The explanation lies in consumption (12.2 kWh per 100 km for the Tesla and 15.4 kWh for the BYD) and in mass: the Seal weighs 1,970 kg, 198 kg more than the Model 3. For anyone driving 400 km (249 miles) between cities, the difference decides between one stop and two.

Fast charging widens the advantage. The Tesla accepts 175 kW and goes from 10 to 80% in 24 minutes; the BYD accepts 110 kW and needs 36 minutes. Twelve extra minutes at every stop on a trip count for more than any gap in home charging, and there is none: both charge at 11 kW on AC.

The Model 3 is also more powerful (208 kW, or 279 hp, against 170 kW, or 228 hp) and quicker: 6.2 s from 0 to 100 km/h against 7.5 s. Its 594-liter trunk beats the Seal's 485 liters, although the BYD is longer (4,800 mm against 4,720 mm) and has a longer wheelbase (2,920 mm against 2,875 mm), which points to a somewhat roomier cabin for rear passengers.

The Seal wins on the standard equipment in its spec sheet. It has a 360° camera, panoramic roof, ventilated seats, Android Auto, Apple CarPlay and V2L, which the Model 3's spec sheet does not record or lists as absent. Its warranty is better too: 6 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles), and 250,000 km (155,000 miles) on the battery, against 4 years or 80,000 km (50,000 miles) and 160,000 km (99,000 miles) for the Tesla. Both have 5 Euro NCAP stars, ISOFIX and a heat pump.

The Model 3 suits people who travel, charge on the road and value range and acceleration. The Seal suits people who drive mainly in the city and prefer a cabin with more equipment, phone connectivity and a longer warranty. This comparison does not take price into account, which could be decisive between two cars this close in size.
