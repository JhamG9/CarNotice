This compares the European specs of the Hyundai Kona Electric 48.6 kWh and the Kia EV3 Standard Range. They are small front-motor SUVs of similar size, but the EV3 has a bigger battery and more power, and that reorders almost the whole list.

The Kona wins on efficiency: 14.6 kWh per 100 km against 14.9 kWh for the EV3, a minimal difference. With 48.6 kWh it is rated at 380 km (236 miles) WLTP, while the Kia, with 58.3 kWh, reaches 436 km (271 miles). The Kona makes good use of a small battery, but the lower capacity costs it 56 km (35 miles).

### Performance and charging

The Kona's motor delivers 99 kW (133 hp) and 255 Nm, and goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 9.9 s. The EV3's delivers 150 kW (201 hp) and 283 Nm, and does it in 7.5 s. Both are enough in the city, but when merging onto a highway with a loaded car the Kia responds with more margin. Top speed changes too: 162 km/h against 170 km/h.

When charging, the difference is 9 minutes. The EV3 goes from 10 to 80% in 30 minutes at a maximum of 100 kW; the Kona takes 39 minutes and accepts up to 74 kW. On AC, both use 11 kW.

### Safety and warranty

These spec sheets include a figure that separates the two directly: the Kona has 4 Euro NCAP stars and the EV3, 5. For anyone carrying children who prioritizes the rating, that weighs heavily. The Kona declares 7 airbags; the Kia's spec sheet does not give its number, so that point cannot be compared. Both include automatic braking, lane keeping and adaptive cruise control, and neither has a blind spot monitor or a 360° camera.

The Kona's general warranty is 5 years with no mileage limit; the EV3's, 7 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles). High-mileage drivers benefit from the first, and people who keep the car longer from the second. The battery has 8 years or 160,000 km (99,000 miles) on both.

### Trunk

The Kona is slightly longer (4,355 mm against 4,300 mm) and narrowly wins on trunk space: 466 liters against 460. The EV3 has a longer wheelbase and V2L, which the Kona does not offer.

This comparison does not take price into account. The Kona suits people who drive mainly in the city and value low consumption. The EV3 suits people who travel and want more power, shorter charging and 5 stars.
