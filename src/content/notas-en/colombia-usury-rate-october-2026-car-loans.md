The **usury rate** for consumer and ordinary loans in Colombia stands at **28.59% effective annual (EA)** for October 2026, according to the certification by the Superintendencia Financiera, the country's financial regulator. It is the legal maximum a bank can charge on a consumer loan, such as one for a car. In September it was 29.24%, so it fell 0.65 percentage points.

## October's figures

| Indicator, consumer and ordinary | September 2026 | October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Standard bank interest rate (IBC) | 19.49% EA | 19.06% EA |
| Usury rate | 29.24% EA | 28.59% EA |

Source: Superintendencia Financiera, published by Portafolio.

The **standard bank interest rate** is the average of what banks charge on these loans. **Usury** is that average multiplied by 1.5: charging above it is a crime. The figure is certified each month.

## What it means for an EV loan

In practice, anyone financing an EV in Colombia pays considerably less than the usury rate. Green loan lines for EVs and hybrids were offered in 2026 at between **12% and 16.6% EA**, according to Portafolio and La República: about 12.5% at Bancolombia, 15.25% at Davivienda and between 16% and 16.65% at Finandina and BBVA. For gasoline cars, the range was between 23.5% and 26.5%, much closer to the ceiling.

| Type of rate | Effective annual | Monthly equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest green loan reported | 12.5% | 0.99% |
| October's standard bank interest rate | 19.06% | 1.46% |
| October's usury rate | 28.59% | 2.12% |

The monthly equivalent rate is our conversion. How to go from one to the other is in [how to read a loan rate](/es/notas/tasa-efectiva-anual-mensual-como-leer-credito-carro/).

## Why usury falls when the central bank raised its rate

October's usury rate is calculated from the loans banks disbursed in previous weeks, so it reflects what has already happened, not what is coming. The Banco de la República, Colombia's central bank, raised its policy rate to **12.25%** on September 30, 2026, on inflation of 6.24%. If banks pass that increase on to new loans, the standard rate and usury could stop falling in the coming months. We explain this in [this article](/es/notas/banco-de-la-republica-tasa-1225-credito-carros/).

## What to do if you plan to finance an EV

**Get quotes from at least three lenders.** The gap between the lowest and highest green rates reported in 2026 was about four points, which on a COP 72 million loan over five years means millions of pesos.

**Compare the total cost, not just the rate.** Insurance, fees and application charges can raise the payment. We review them in [the hidden costs of a car loan](/es/notas/costos-ocultos-credito-vehicular/).

**Be wary of offers near usury.** If an EV buyer is offered a rate above 20% EA, they are paying what they would pay for a gasoline car or a general-purpose loan.

> Usury is a legal ceiling, not a price reference. For an EV, a good rate in 2026 is closer to half the usury rate than to the usury rate itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the usury rate for October 2026?

28.59% effective annual for consumer and ordinary loans.

### What happens if a bank charges more than the usury rate?

Charging interest above the usury rate is a crime in Colombia, and customers can complain to the lender and to the Superintendencia Financiera.

### How much do banks charge for an EV loan?

In 2026, between 12% and 16.6% EA on the green loan lines reported by the press. Confirm the current rate with each lender.
