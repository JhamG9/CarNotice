Two loans at the same rate can end up costing different amounts. The difference lies in what is added around the loan: the insurance the bank requires, the car paperwork and the lien that stays on record until the last payment. This is the list of what to ask before signing an EV loan in Colombia.

## 1. Comprehensive insurance

Almost all banks require the financed car to have valid comprehensive insurance during the loan, with the bank as beneficiary. It is the largest additional cost.

According to a 2026 study by the platform Seguro Canguro, the cheapest offer for an EV worth COP 90 to 120 million cost about **2.07% of the car's value a year**. On a COP 89.99 million EV, that is about **COP 1.86 million a year**, or about COP 155,000 a month if added to the payment. The cheapest premiums found were COP 2,343,700 a year for a Tesla Model Y, COP 2,273,545 for a BYD Yuan Up and COP 1,741,764 for an MG S5. We explain this in [how much it costs to insure an EV](/es/notas/seguro-carro-electrico-colombia-2026-costo/).

Ask whether you can take out the insurance yourself or must take the one the bank offers. If you can choose, getting your own quote is usually cheaper.

## 2. Borrower's life insurance

Consumer loans usually include life insurance that pays off the balance if the borrower dies or is disabled. It is charged monthly with the payment, and its value depends on the balance and on age. Ask for it to be shown separately in the simulation.

## 3. The lien on the car

The financed car is **pledged**: it has a lien in the bank's favor, recorded in RUNT, the national vehicle registry, until the loan is paid. While it exists, the car cannot be freely sold or transferred. When you finish paying, you must ask the bank for a clearance letter and **lift the lien** at the transit office, which has its own fee: in Bogotá it was COP 126,200 in the latest table published by Valora Analitik, from 2022, and it varies by city.

## 4. Registration, RUNT and SOAT

These are not loan costs, but they are paid when buying and sometimes financed within the same loan:

| Procedure | Cost in Bogotá in 2026 |
|---|---|
| Car registration | COP 708,200 |
| RUNT entry | COP 22,100 |
| SOAT, the mandatory accident insurance | With a 10% discount on the premium for EVs |

Bogotá transit fees in force since January 1, 2026, according to Infobae. Many dealers include registration in the published price; Tesla's 0% financing, for example, advertises prices with registration included. Ask what the price includes.

## 5. If you finance the extras, you pay interest on them

If insurance, registration or accessories are added to the loan amount, you pay interest on them for the whole term. A COP 1.86 million insurance policy financed over five years at 12.5% effective annual ends up costing considerably more than paid in cash.

## How to compare offers

Ask each lender for three figures in writing: the **effective annual rate**, the **total monthly payment with insurance included** and the **total amount payable** over the loan. With those three you can compare any offer. How to read the rate is in [this article](/es/notas/tasa-efectiva-anual-mensual-como-leer-credito-carro/).

> An offer with the lowest rate and expensive mandatory insurance can end up costing more than one with a rate half a point higher and freedom to choose the insurance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is comprehensive insurance mandatory on a car loan?

In practice, almost all banks require it while the car is pledged. SOAT is mandatory by law for every car.

### What is a lien on a car?

A guarantee in the bank's favor, recorded in RUNT until the loan is paid. You then have to lift it at the transit office.

### How much does it cost to register a new car in Bogotá?

COP 708,200 for registration and COP 22,100 for the RUNT entry, at 2026 rates.
