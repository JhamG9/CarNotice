This compares the entry versions sold in Colombia: the Geely EX2 Max and the Kia EV2 Air. They are close rivals. Both have five seats, are about 4.1 meters long (4,135 mm for the Geely, 4,060 mm for the Kia) and use LFP batteries of around 40 kWh.

On range, both spec sheets include a WLTP figure: 325 km (202 miles) for the EX2, with 39.4 kWh, and 312 km (194 miles) for the EV2, with 42.2 kWh. The 13 km (8 mile) gap is small, and the EX2 achieves it with 2.8 kWh less battery. The Kia's spec sheet declares 15.1 kWh/100 km; the Geely's does not include that figure.

### Power and charging

The Kia wins here. It delivers 108.1 kW (145 hp) and 250 Nm, and goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 8.7 s; the EX2 delivers 85 kW (114 hp) and 150 Nm, and takes 11.5 s. The difference is felt when merging onto a highway or climbing with a loaded car. Top speed also favors the Kia: 161 km/h against 130 km/h.

Fast charging follows the same order. The EV2 accepts 100 kW and goes from 10 to 80% in 29 minutes. The EX2 accepts 70 kW and goes from 30 to 80% in 21 minutes; since the stretches differ, the times are not comparable. The Kia uses a CCS1 connector and the Geely CCS2, something to check against the stations in your area. On AC, the Kia accepts 11 kW and the Geely 6.6 kW.

### Space, equipment and warranty

The EX2 gives more space: a 375-liter trunk and 70 extra liters up front, against 362 and 15 liters in the Kia, and a wheelbase of 2,650 mm against 2,560 mm. It has a 14.6-inch screen, a 360° camera, V2L and a wireless charger. The Kia adds seven airbags against six and lane keeping, which the Geely's spec sheet lacks; both declare autonomous braking and adaptive cruise control. The Kia records no 360° camera and its spec sheet has no V2L data. The Kia's warranty is 8 years or 160,000 km (99,000 miles), and the Geely's, 6 years or 150,000 km (93,000 miles).

### Who each one is for

The Kia suits people who drive on the highway and value motor response, faster charging and a longer warranty. The Geely suits people looking for more trunk space, technology and a 360° camera for daily use. This comparison does not take price into account.
