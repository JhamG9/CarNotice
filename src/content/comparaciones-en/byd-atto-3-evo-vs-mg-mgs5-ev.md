This comparison uses the European specs of the entry trims: the BYD Atto 3 Evo RWD Design and the 49 kWh MG MGS5 EV. Battery and equipment change in other markets. They are close in size, at 4,455 mm long for the BYD and 4,476 mm for the MG, both compact five-seat SUVs with rear-wheel drive.

The difference that matters most is the battery. The Atto 3 Evo carries 74.8 kWh and is rated at 510 km (317 miles) WLTP; the MGS5 EV has 49 kWh and 340 km (211 miles) WLTP. That is 170 km (106 miles) more for the BYD, the margin between a round trip to a nearby city without charging and having to plan a stop. Rated consumption is almost identical (16.4 kWh/100 km for the BYD, 16.6 for the MG), so the advantage comes from battery size, not from better efficiency.

### Power and charging

The BYD delivers 230 kW (308 hp) and goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 5.5 s; the MG delivers 125 kW (168 hp) and needs 8 s. Overtaking on the highway takes less effort in the BYD, which also reaches 180 km/h against 170 km/h.

On DC, the BYD accepts 220 kW and goes from 10 to 80% in 25 minutes; the MG accepts 120 kW and takes 26. The times nearly tie because the MG's battery is much smaller, so in an equivalent stop the BYD recovers more energy and more distance. On AC it is 11 kW against 6.6 kW: at home or on a wall charger, the BYD fills up in fewer hours.

### Space and equipment

The BYD's trunk holds 490 liters plus 95 liters up front; the MG's holds 453 liters. The BYD has a heat pump, heated and ventilated seats, a 360° camera and a 15.6-inch screen. The MG's spec sheet lists no heat pump, no heated seats, no 360° camera and a 12.8-inch screen. In winter, a heat pump cuts range less than resistive heating does. The MG answers with 5 Euro NCAP stars, a figure the BYD's spec sheet does not include.

### Who each one is for

The BYD suits people who travel often, charge on the highway or want full equipment from the entry trim. The MG works for people who use the car mainly in and around the city and prefer safety with a proven rating. This comparison does not take price into account, which could tip the balance toward the MG.
