This compares the entry versions sold in Colombia: the XPeng L03 PowerX Plus (model year 2027) and the Zeekr X Sport RWD (2026). The underlying difference is technology. The Zeekr X is a battery EV. The L03 is a range-extended EV: a 1,500 cc, 4-cylinder gasoline engine generates electricity to extend its range.

### Range: two different tests

One figure cannot be compared with the other without caution. The Zeekr is rated at 330 km (205 miles) on the WLTP cycle with a 51 kWh battery. The L03 is rated at 215 km (134 miles) of electric range on the WLTP cycle with 37.2 kWh, and 1,017 km (632 miles) of total range counting its 42-liter tank. Only the electric stretch is comparable with the Zeekr, and there the Zeekr wins by 115 km (71 miles). In exchange, the L03 can be refueled: for anyone covering long roads with no fast charging nearby, it is the only one of the two that does not depend on a charger.

### Charging and connection

The Zeekr charges at up to 130 kW and goes from 10 to 80% in 20 minutes; the L03 reaches 112 kW and covers the same stretch in 20 minutes. With a smaller battery, 112 kW is enough for the L03 to match that time. On AC the Zeekr accepts 11 kW and the L03 6.6 kW, so overnight charging at home is faster in the Zeekr.

### Performance and space

The Zeekr goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 5.6 s with 200 kW (268 hp) and 343 Nm; the L03 does it in 6.8 s with 180 kW (241 hp) and 264 Nm. Both have rear-wheel drive. The L03 is considerably larger: 4,650 mm long with a 531-liter trunk, against 4,432 mm and 362 liters plus 30 liters up front for the Zeekr. It is heavier too, at 1,935 kg against 1,855 kg.

### Safety and equipment

The Zeekr declares 5 Euro NCAP stars, a figure the L03 does not have. Both carry 7 airbags, autonomous braking, adaptive cruise, lane keeping and blind spot warning. Only the L03 declares a 360° camera, heated and ventilated seats, and V2L; the Zeekr's spec sheet has no data on any of them.

### Who each one suits

The Zeekr X suits people who use the car in the city and on medium trips, want the strongest performance and have somewhere to charge. The L03 suits families who make long trips, need trunk space and do not want to plan every recharge. This comparison does not take price into account.
